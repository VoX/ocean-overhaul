Features

What Ocean Overhaul adds to the deep.

Megalodon & Apex Gear

Hunt the Megalodon, a colossal apex boss shark, for guaranteed Megalodon Teeth, then forge the Abyssal Fang — an apex sword a full tier above Tidal (9 attack damage, 2200 durability, repaired with teeth).

The Abyssal Trench

Deep oceans now grow bioluminescent plankton and hydrothermal vents; giant clams hide guaranteed pearls, and the Megalodon and Abyssal Lurker concentrate down in the dark. Finding the deep is the deep-content gate.

Harpoon & Diving Kit

Throw a Harpoon that yanks your target back toward you and loyalty-returns to your hand. Equip Flippers (swim speed), an Oxygen Tank (water breathing) and a Deep-Sea Helmet (night vision) to make the depths traversable.

Aquariums

Bucket the mod's Reef Fish and Jellyfish — color variants preserved — and place an Aquarium block to watch your catch swim around inside a glass tank.

Tidal Gear

A full Tidal armor and tool set crafted from ocean materials, granting water-breathing and built for life below the surface.

Ocean Building Sets

Driftwood wood-set, abyssal coral, prismarine, pearl and sea-glass blocks — with stairs, slabs, walls and chiseled variants for true underwater builds.

Mobs

New creatures from the shallows to the trench.

Megalodon

A massive apex boss shark that patrols the deep ocean and drops guaranteed Megalodon Teeth.

Abyssal Lurker

A bioluminescent anglerfish of the depths, its lure glowing in the dark.

Reef Fish

Schooling tropical reef fish that bring color to shallow waters.

Jellyfish

Drifting jellyfish in five distinct color variants.

Installation

Up and running in four steps.

  1. Install Fabric — Install Minecraft 1.21.1 and the Fabric Loader.
  2. Add Fabric API — Download Fabric API for 1.21.1 and drop it in your mods/ folder.
  3. Add Ocean Overhaul — Grab the latest ocean-overhaul jar from the Releases page and drop it in mods/ too.
  4. Dive in — Launch Minecraft with the Fabric profile, start a world, and head for deep water.
